# Never List

1. Never invent a product specification, EAN, price, stock level or delivery promise.
2. Never publish placeholder product data as if it were verified.
3. Never use customer data or live payment data in a design connector.
4. Never make a design decision that contradicts the current specs or principles.
5. Never introduce a new colour, font size, spacing value or component variant without recording and verifying it in the source-of-truth files.
6. Never add a mechanic that makes the customer believe they can complete checkout on MYOFFICESHOP when checkout actually happens on eMAG.
7. Never use fake testimonials, fake scarcity or unsupported performance claims.
8. Never hide the product quantity or the destination of the purchase CTA.
9. Never mix brands or product variants in a way that makes the offer ambiguous.
10. Never treat an unverified assumption as a requirement.
