# Design Principles Skill

## Job
Judge whether a MYOFFICESHOP design supports the customer's decision without inventing information.

## Inputs
- day-one.md
- never-list.md
- skills/specs/SKILL.md
- the screen or screenshot being reviewed

## Principles
1. Clarity before decoration: the customer must understand the product, quantity and next step.
2. One source of truth: use only verified values from the specs and product data.
3. Marketplace honesty: clearly distinguish browsing/information from the eMAG purchase action.
4. Reduce decision friction: remove unnecessary steps rather than adding interface complexity.
5. Trust through specificity: use real product facts and avoid unsupported claims.

## Output
A pass/fail judgement with the principle that was applied and the reason.

## Refusal
Do not approve a design just because it looks polished. Fail it when a customer could misunderstand what they are buying or where the purchase happens.
