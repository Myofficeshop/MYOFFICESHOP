# Glossary

- **Ream / top**: 500 sheets of paper.
- **Box / bax**: 5 reams, or 2,500 sheets.
- **eMAG Marketplace**: The main marketplace through which the current business sells.
- **SKU**: The seller's internal product code.
- **EAN**: The manufacturer/supplier barcode used to identify a product.
- **Buy Button**: The main eMAG offer placement that drives the purchase action.
- **FBE**: Fulfilment by eMAG.
- **AWB**: Courier shipping label/document.
- **TeoShip**: The intended automation layer for orders, stock, price and AWB workflows.
- **Product family**: A grouping of product variants, for example the same paper in different quantities.
