# Day One

## Venture
MYOFFICESHOP / Sebastian Global Trade SRL is an online office-supplies business focused on A4 copier paper and related office consumables. The current website is a static landing page that directs customers to eMAG listings.

## User
The primary customer is a Romanian office buyer who needs reliable copier paper and wants a simple way to understand the offer and purchase it. The business also needs to support B2B-style buyers who purchase in larger quantities.

## Problem
The business needs to increase sales and profitability while reducing avoidable dependency on a single marketplace and manual operational work.

## Current offer
- Maestro Mondi A4 copier paper, 80 g/m²
- Navigator Universal A4 copier paper, 80 g/m²
- Sold as individual reams and 5-ream boxes where the listing supports it

## Current channels and operations
- eMAG Marketplace is the main sales channel.
- myofficeshop.ro is a supporting website/landing page.
- Romanian Paper Distribution is the main supplier.
- Sameday is used for delivery.
- TeoShip is intended to automate orders, stock, prices and AWB workflows.
- Fulfilment is currently handled by the seller rather than FBE.

## Business constraint
Do not invent product facts, prices, stock, delivery promises, manufacturer data, EANs or legal claims. Verify them from the supplier, product packaging, eMAG data or another authoritative source before publishing.

## Design job
Design should make it easy for a customer to understand the office-paper offer, compare quantity/value, and move to the correct eMAG purchase path.

## Success
A customer should be able to understand what is being sold, who it is for, what quantity they are getting, and how to purchase it without needing to ask for missing information.

## Out of scope
- Checkout inside the MYOFFICESHOP site
- Inventing product specifications or prices
- Replacing eMAG as the transactional marketplace without a validated business case
